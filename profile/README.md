### Overview & Current Status

**RHDT IT** (currently in the architecture phase) is an upcoming it-services provider based in Germany.

Before launching commercial operations, our primary focus is the development of a fully deterministic, zero-touch automation stack. Our goal is to provide small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) with resilient software, sovereign European cloud services, and strict *Security & Privacy by Design* architectures—delivering enterprise-grade security with minimal operational overhead.
